# Iconoclast Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: September 25, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how Iconoclast (“Iconoclast,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects and uses information when you interact with the Iconoclast Discord bot. 
Using Iconoclast or a Discord server in which Iconoclast has been added to may result in the processing of information as described below. 

## 1. Information We Process

Depending on the features used or enabled in a server, Iconoclast may process or store information including:

* Discord User, Server, Channel, Role, and Message IDs;
* usernames, display names, roles, and other basic Discord account or server information;
* message content when required for features such as moderation, automod, logging, commands, or other configured functionality;
* command interactions, bot usage, economy, levels, moderation records, and other data created through Iconoclast.

Iconoclast only accesses Discord data available to it through Discord's API and permissions granted to the bot.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information is used only as needed to:

* provide and maintain Iconoclast's features;
* store user or server settings and progress;
* moderate servers and prevent abuse;
* investigate security incidents or violations;
* diagnose errors and improve the service.

## 3. Data Sharing

We do **not sell or rent personal information**.

Information may be processed by service providers or infrastructure used to operate Iconoclast, or disclosed when necessary in order to comply with law, protect users, prevent abuse, or protect Iconoclast and its services.

## 4. Data Retention and Security

We take responsible measures to protect stored information and restrict access to authorized purposes.

Information is retained only for as long as it is required to provide Iconoclast's features, maintain security, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations.

## 5. Your Data

You may request access to or deletion of data associated with your Discord account by contacting us. We may require reasonable verification before processing a request.

Some information may be retained where necessary for security, abuse prevention, legal obligations, or other legitimate purposes.

## 6. Discord

Iconoclast is an independent third-party Discord application and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Discord Inc.

Your use of Discord remains subject to Discord's own Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and other applicable policies.

## 7. Changes and Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy when Iconoclast or its data practices change. The latest published version will apply.
